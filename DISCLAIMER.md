# DISCLAIMER

## About This Project

Project 3DU (Three-Dimensional Universe Update Model) is an independent, open research notebook exploring a speculative framework for understanding the universe.

The ideas presented here are intended to stimulate discussion, encourage critical thinking, and inspire new questions. They should **not** be interpreted as established scientific theory unless explicitly supported by experimental evidence.

---

## Project Philosophy

This repository documents an ongoing journey rather than a finished destination.

Ideas contained here may evolve, be revised, or even be replaced as new evidence, better reasoning, or constructive criticism becomes available.

Changing our minds in the presence of better evidence is considered progress, not failure.

---

## Scientific Approach

- Respect established experimental evidence.
- Distinguish clearly between observations, hypotheses, and speculation.
- Search for testable predictions whenever possible.
- Welcome constructive criticism and alternative explanations.
- Record both successful and unsuccessful ideas as part of the research process.
