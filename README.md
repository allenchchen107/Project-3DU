# Project 3DU

## Three-Dimensional Universe Film Playing Model

> *Exploring whether the universe may be understood as a sequence of discrete three-dimensional states whose rapid evolution gives rise to the continuous reality we experience.*

---

## Vision

Project 3DU is an open exploration of one fundamental question:

**What if the universe is fundamentally discrete rather than continuous?**

Instead of assuming that space and time evolve continuously, the 3DU model asks whether the entire universe could be viewed as a sequence of complete three-dimensional "frames" that update at an extremely high frequency.

If the update frequency is sufficiently high, continuity, motion, causality, electromagnetic propagation, and even consciousness may emerge naturally from this underlying process.

The purpose of this project is **not** to challenge established physics, but to investigate whether a different underlying interpretation could eventually explain why today's successful physical theories work so well.

---

## Project Origin

Project 3DU originated from ideas developed by **Allen Chen**, inspired by many years of engineering experience, curiosity about the nature of reality, and a desire to connect concepts across physics, information, and consciousness.

The project has been developed through extensive discussions with **ChatGPT ("PIZ")**, which has served as a collaborative research assistant—helping organize ideas, ask critical questions, improve clarity, identify possible connections to existing scientific theories, and draft documentation.

The ideas presented here remain speculative and exploratory. The goal is to build them carefully, evaluate them honestly, and welcome constructive criticism from anyone interested in fundamental physics.

---

## Core Questions

Project 3DU attempts to explore questions such as:

- Is spacetime fundamentally discrete?
- Why is the speed of light constant?
- Can classical continuity emerge from discrete updates?
- Can quantum behavior arise naturally from finite update processes?
- Is consciousness itself a rapidly refreshed sequence of discrete cognitive states?
- Could information be more fundamental than matter?

---

## Guiding Principles

Project 3DU follows several principles throughout its development.

- Respect established experimental evidence.
- Clearly distinguish observations from hypotheses.
- Build ideas step by step.
- Revise or discard ideas whenever evidence requires.
- Prefer questions over conclusions.

---

## Research Philosophy

One idea has become central to this project:

> **Knowledge is never absolute. Decisions are made when confidence exceeds the threshold appropriate to the consequences.**

At the same time, we recognize an even deeper principle:

> **Our ultimate goal is 100% certainty.**
>
> We should also be grateful when evidence reaches 99.99% confidence, because complete certainty may be fundamentally unattainable if the total amount of information in the universe is infinite.

This philosophy encourages curiosity while remaining intellectually honest.
