## Welcome

Project **3DU** is an open engineering research notebook dedicated to exploring one fundamental question:

> **Could space, time, matter, energy, fields, and consciousness emerge from a deeper universe update process?**

The name **3DU** stands for **Three-Dimensional Universe Update Model**.

The central hypothesis proposes that the universe may be represented as a sequence of complete three-dimensional states ("frames"), where the continual updating of these states gives rise to what we experience as time, motion, physical interactions, and consciousness.

This project is **not** intended to replace established physics.

Instead, it asks whether today's successful physical theories might themselves emerge from a deeper computational architecture.

---

# Why This Project Exists

Throughout history, many scientific breakthroughs have unified ideas that once appeared unrelated.

Electricity and magnetism became electromagnetism.

Space and time became spacetime.

Today we continue searching for deeper connections among quantum mechanics, relativity, gravitation, information, and computation.

Project 3DU follows that same spirit of curiosity.

Rather than beginning with certainty, it begins with questions.

---

# Research Philosophy

Project 3DU is founded on four principles.

### Curiosity

Questions are often more valuable than immediate answers.

### Evidence

Confidence should grow from observation, experiment, and careful reasoning.

### Humility

Every conclusion remains open to revision whenever stronger evidence or better explanations become available.

### Collaboration

Progress comes from people thinking together.

Constructive criticism, alternative viewpoints, mathematical analysis, and experimental ideas are always welcome.

Our objective is **not to defend ideas**, but to improve them—or replace them—whenever doing so brings us closer to reality.

# Guiding Principle

We regard **complete understanding** as the ultimate destination of scientific exploration.

At the same time, we recognize that complete knowledge may be fundamentally unattainable when the available raw data is incomplete or inaccessible.

Practical decisions therefore rely on confidence rather than absolute certainty.

The confidence required depends upon the consequences of the decision being made.

# Long-Term Goals

The objectives of Project 3DU include

- Developing a consistent conceptual framework for the 3DU model.
- Comparing the model with established physical theories.
- Identifying both agreements and contradictions.
- Proposing mathematical formulations.
- Designing experiments capable of distinguishing the model from existing theories.
- Encouraging open discussion and collaboration.
