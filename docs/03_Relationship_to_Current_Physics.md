# 03 Relationship to Current Physics

This project respects existing physics and seeks possible relationships rather than replacement.

Topics for future study:

- Maxwell's Equations
- Special and General Relativity
- Quantum Mechanics
- Planck Scale
- Information Theory
- Digital Physics
- Cellular Automata
- Wheeler's 'It from Bit'

Questions include whether existing theories could emerge as approximations of a deeper update process.
