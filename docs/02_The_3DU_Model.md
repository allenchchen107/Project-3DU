# 02 The 3DU Model

## Working Hypothesis

Assume the universe consists of complete 3D states updated at an unknown frequency.

A 'frame' contains the complete state of the 3D universe.

The apparent flow of time is the succession of frame updates.

Motion, fields and causality emerge from differences between consecutive frames.

Without frame updates, no time existed, no energy, no matter can be appeared.

## Early ideas discussed

- Consciousness may also consist of rapidly refreshed discrete states.
- Classical continuity may emerge from sufficiently high update frequency.
- The speed of light may be related to the underlying update mechanism.
- Electromagnetic propagation may provide clues about the update process.

Status: Working hypothesis.
